Mission & Architecture Principles

Democratizing Intelligent Security

Why we believe modern intrusion prevention must be self-hosted, mathematically transparent, and accessible to every developer.

🔒

1. Zero Data Leakage

Traditional cloud WAFs require routing your sensitive customer payload traffic through third-party servers. STEALTHWALL runs 100% in-process or on your own private infrastructure with zero external telemetry calls.

🔍

2. Mathematical Transparency

Black-box AI models that can't explain why a request was blocked lead to broken production APIs. STEALTHWALL exposes exact statistical feature calculations and anomaly scores for full operational confidence.

⚡

3. Zero Cloud Lock-In

Deploy anywhere: bare-metal Linux servers, AWS EC2, DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Docker clusters, or Next.js edge runtimes. You maintain complete ownership of your security posture.

Reporting Guidelines

If you discover a security issue or bypass in the STEALTHWALL engine, please send an encrypted or direct report to chinmayshinde247@gmail.com. Please do not open public GitHub issues for unpatched vulnerabilities.

We commit to acknowledging all security reports within 24 hours and shipping verified patches to PyPI, npm, and GitHub within 48 hours.
